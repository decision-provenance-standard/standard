| `decision_statement` | Under the rule recorded in DR-2026-0003, the Founding Steward classifies v1.3 (reading edition rev. 11) of the text, released with reference files 5.2.0, as a minor release. Every rule change in it (DR-2026-0016 to DR-2026-0019) adds a signal for a requirement v1.2 already stated, states the compatibility promise as Appendix G §G.7.6 does, removes a way to fail, adds a way to pass, or turns a requirement into a recommendation, and none makes a record or Charter valid under v1.2 (reading edition rev. 10) invalid. The reference files now accept what the text accepts, and the one shape 5.1.2 accepted that 5.2.0 rejects is a record at `drafted` or later with no `altitude`, which the text has required from the draft state in every published edition. |
