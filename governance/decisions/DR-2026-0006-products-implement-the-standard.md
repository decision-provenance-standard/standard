| `decision_statement` | Products and tools say that they implement the Decision Provenance Standard, as in "Our decision-record tool implements the Decision Provenance Standard™", and are never described as conformant: a Conformance Level belongs to an organization's Charter (§7), and a product claims none. §11.1 and Appendix G §G.11.3 now permit the "implements" wording in place of rev. 8's "Standard-conformant tooling" and "is conformant with", and the same wording replaces "conformant" wherever the text describes a reference implementation, a decision-record formatter, a schedule-of-records exporter or a tool's dispatch state machine (§3.2, §6.2.4, §6.3, §6.4.1, §7.4.1 and Companion C §C.4.2). Under the rule recorded in DR-2026-0003, the Founding Steward classifies this change as minor, and v1.1 (reading edition rev. 9), classified as a minor release in DR-2026-0005, stays a minor release with it included: the change alters only how products and tools are described, in the text and in the permitted uses of the Standard's name, and no field, allowed value or rule for records or Charters, so no record or Charter valid under rev. 8 becomes invalid. |
| `context_at_decision` | • Rev. 8 §11.1 permitted a vendor to identify its product as "Standard-conformant tooling", with the example "Our decision-record tool is conformant with the Decision Provenance Standard™", and Appendix G §G.11.3 permitted "Standard-conformant tooling".<br>• Section 7 grades Conformance Levels against a Charter, and the organization self-declares its Level; the text defines no Level, and no conformance, for a product.<br>• Rev. 8 already said that a vendor's product references the Standard "as the open standard the product implements", and "Stamp the tool, not the org" (§11.1, §G.11.3).<br>• Rev. 8 also called a reference implementation, a decision-record formatter, a schedule-of-records exporter and an implementation's dispatch state machine "conformant", in §3.2, §6.2.4, §6.3, §6.4.1, the §7.4.1 Level 3 table and Companion C §C.4.2.<br>• A reader who sees a product called "conformant" can take it as a claim to a Level, or as a sign that the Charters run on it are conformant, which the Standard does not say. |

Chosen: it matches what rev. 8 already said about the product implementing the Standard, and it changes no record or Charter.<br>• Add "conformant" for products to the §11.1 list of prohibited uses.
